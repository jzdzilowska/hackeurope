# SME Financial Dashboard

An AI-powered financial dashboard for small and medium enterprises, built at **HackEurope** (FinTech track).

## What it does

SME owners connect their bank accounts, and the dashboard gives them a complete financial picture — spending breakdowns, revenue trends, cash flow forecasts, and AI-generated cost-saving advice — without needing an accountant.

## Key Features

- **Bank account linking** with automatic transaction sync and categorization
- **AI financial consultant** — chat about your finances, get specific cost-saving suggestions with estimated savings
- **Invoice parsing** — automatically detects and extracts invoices from Gmail (photos, PDFs, emails)
- **Cash flow forecasting** based on transaction history and upcoming invoice due dates
- **Invoice payments** directly from the dashboard
- **Voice summaries** — hear your daily/weekly financial overview spoken aloud
